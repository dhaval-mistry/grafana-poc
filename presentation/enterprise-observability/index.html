Business Need

Why Observability Matters

Reactive model

Customer reports the problem first

  • Signals are fragmented across browser, API, and database layers.
  • Support and engineering spend time rebuilding context.
  • Leadership sees technical noise instead of customer impact.
Target model

Teams see impact and context earlier

  • Customer journey and service health are connected.
  • One correlation ID carries the investigation context.
  • Each audience gets the view required for its decision.
Goal: reduce the distance between customer impact and confident action.

Journey Context

Connect the Customer Journey Without Exposing Sensitive Data

SESSION ID

Browser lifecycle

Provides continuity across authenticated browser activity without representing customer identity.

CORRELATION ID

One business journey

Connects a login or transfer journey across related operational records and requests.

TRACE ID

One distributed execution

Shows the technical execution path across browser, API, business spans, and database calls.

Context for investigation without customer identity

Governance

Privacy and Security by Design

EXCLUDED

Sensitive banking data

  • No passwords or access tokens
  • No account numbers or transfer amounts
  • No request/response bodies or SQL parameters
CONTROLLED

Safe telemetry design

  • Bounded metric dimensions
  • Searchable context kept out of metric labels
  • Approved log and trace attributes only
USEFUL

Operationally actionable

  • Journey stage and outcome
  • Failure category and route
  • Correlation and distributed trace context
Observability should improve investigation without creating a second copy of sensitive banking data.
Privacy is part of the architecture, not an afterthought
